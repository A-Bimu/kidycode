The Owl Check is used throughout the course:

1. Reproduce the problem.
2. Say what should happen.
3. Observe what actually happens.
4. Trace one step at a time.
5. Isolate the smallest incorrect part.
6. Change one thing.
7. Test again.
8. Explain the repair.

## Mastery rules

Each stage has three evidence stars:

- Understand: the learner passes the lesson checks.
- Build: the boss project works against its requirements.
- Explain: the learner explains one decision and one test or repair.

Retries are unlimited. Hints never remove points. A stage is mastered only when all three stars are earned. Digital safety, debugging and the final project cannot be skipped.
