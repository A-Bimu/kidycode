KidyCode V4 · colour system

Four learner palettes, one admin system

The four age groups do not share a palette. They share a discipline: a near-white or cool ground, one committed brand hue, one or two energetic accents used as fills (never as small text on white), and no green anywhere. Every pair below was measured, not eyeballed — docs/v4/palette-contrast.mjs re-runs the check and currently reports 56 of 56 pairs passing.

Read the ratios as instructions, not scores. An accent under 3:1 against white is fine as a fill — a chip, a bar, a star — as long as the text sitting on it is dark ink (all such pairs clear 4.5:1, e.g. #131A2E on #FFB020 = 11.1:1). None of those accents is ever used for small text on a white ground; where an accent hue is needed as text, the palette carries a darkened variant (#B3490A, #0B6FA8, #C41F42, #0369A1) and those all clear 4.5:1.

Admin dashboard — gold refinement only, structure untouched

What changes

The approved admin structure — sidebar, overview, curriculum explorer, learner preview, analytics, notification centre, settings — stays exactly as reviewed. Only the gold changes: from one flat mustard (#D4A63A) to a luminous metallic ramp with a highlight edge, used sparingly on the top bar rule, primary actions and the one figure that matters most on a screen.

Type, shape and motion per group

A · 10–12

Baloo 2 headings · Nunito Sans body · JetBrains Mono code. 22px radii, soft lift, dashed trail. Motion: a 200 ms pop on completion, star twinkle; off under reduced-motion.

B · 13–15

Poppins headings · Inter Tight body. 14px radii, hairlines, flat panels. Motion: 140 ms cross-fade between Brief / Make / Check; no celebration animation.

C · 16–18

Space Grotesk headings · Inter Tight body · JetBrains Mono for everything code. 10px radii, dark surfaces. Motion: 120 ms panel transitions; the terminal reveals output line by line at 60 ms.

D · Adults

IBM Plex Serif headings · IBM Plex Sans body · IBM Plex Mono code. 8–12px radii, near-flat. Motion: 120 ms opacity only. No decorative animation at all.
