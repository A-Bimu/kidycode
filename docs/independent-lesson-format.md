# KidyCode Independent Lesson Format

KidyCode lessons for ages 10 to 12 are designed so a child can complete them without a teacher sitting beside them.

Every lesson should answer four questions clearly:

1. What am I making?
2. What should I do now?
3. How do I know it worked?
4. What can I try if I get stuck?

## Lesson flow

1. Story mission
2. Today's goal
3. Short reading
4. Worked example
5. Your turn activity
6. Bug-finding challenge
7. Three-question check
8. Small creation
9. Explain what you learned
10. Mark the lesson complete

## Writing rules

- Write at an age-10 reading level.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Introduce one main idea at a time.
- Explain new words where they first appear.
- Let the learner try before showing the full answer.
- Use gradual hints that preserve the child's thinking.
- Give immediate and encouraging feedback.
- End with something the learner can make or demonstrate.
